# Laws of Cricket

Maintained by the Marylebone Cricket Club (MCC), the Laws of Cricket govern international and domestic play worldwide.

## Core Fundamentals
* **Pitch & Stumps**: Played on a 22-yard pitch with three stumps and two bails at each end.
* **Match Structure**: Two teams of 11 players alternate between batting and fielding.
* **Deliveries**: A standard over consists of 6 legal deliveries.

## Key Dismissal Types
1. **Bowled**: The ball strikes the wicket directly or off the bat/body and dislodges a bail.
2. **Caught**: A fielder catches a ball hit by the bat before it hits the ground.
3. **Leg Before Wicket (LBW)**: The ball hits the batter's body in line with the stumps when it would have otherwise hit the wicket (and did not hit the bat first).
4. **Run Out**: A fielder dislodges the bails with the ball while the batter is outside their crease attempting a run.
5. **Stumped**: The wicket-keeper dislodges the bails when the batter is out of their crease while not attempting a run.

## Extra Deliveries & Penalties
* **No Ball**: Overstepping the crease, dangerous height, or illegal bowling action. Results in 1 run penalty + free hit in limited-overs formats.
* **Wide Ball**: A delivery passing out of the batter's reach. Results in 1 run penalty and an extra delivery.
