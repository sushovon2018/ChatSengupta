# History of Test Cricket

Test cricket is the oldest and longest format of the game, played over up to five days with two innings per team.

## Key Milestones
* **1877**: The first official Test match took place between Australia and England at the Melbourne Cricket Ground (MCG). Australia won by 45 runs.
* **1882**: The Ashes tradition was born following Australia's first Test win on English soil at The Oval, prompting a satirical obituary for English cricket.
* **1932–33**: The infamous **Bodyline** series, where England used targeted fast bowling at batters' bodies to restrict Don Bradman.
* **1977**: World Series Cricket (Kerry Packer) disrupted traditional cricket structures, introducing day-night matches, colored clothing, and commercial broadcast standards.
* **2015**: The first Day/Night Test match played with a pink ball took place between Australia and New Zealand in Adelaide.
