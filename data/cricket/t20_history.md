# History of Twenty20 (T20) Cricket

Designed to offer a high-intensity, short-duration match format lasting around 3 hours.

## Key Milestones
* **2003**: The England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB) formally introduced the T20 format for domestic competition to boost spectator attendance.
* **2007**: The inaugural ICC World Twenty20 took place in South Africa, won by India in a dramatic final against Pakistan.
* **2008**: Launch of the Indian Premier League (IPL), revolutionizing cricket commerce, player auctions, and franchise league expansion globally.
