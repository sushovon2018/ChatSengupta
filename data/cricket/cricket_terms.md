# Glossary of Cricket Terms

* **Doosra**: An off-spinner's delivery that turns from leg to off (away from the right-handed batter).
* **Duckworth-Lewis-Stern (DLS) Method**: A mathematical formula used to recalculate target scores in rain-affected limited-overs matches.
* **Googly**: A leg-spinner's delivery that turns from off to leg (into the right-handed batter).
* **Reverse Swing**: A phenomenon where an older ball moves toward the shiny side rather than away, typically achieved at higher speeds.
* **Yorker**: A delivery that lands directly at or near the batter's feet/crease line.
