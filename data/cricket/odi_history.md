# History of One Day Internationals (ODIs)

ODI cricket limits each side to 50 overs (originally 60) and is completed in a single day.

## Evolution Timeline
* **January 5, 1971**: The first official ODI was played by chance at the Melbourne Cricket Ground after a Test match was rained out. Australia played England in a 40-over (8 balls/over) match.
* **1975**: The inaugural ICC Cricket World Cup took place in England, won by the West Indies.
* **1992**: World Cup in Australia & New Zealand introduced white balls, colored uniforms, day-night fixtures under floodlights, and field restriction rules.
* **2000s–Present**: Introduction of Powerplays, mandatory boundary adjustments, and two new balls per innings (2011).
